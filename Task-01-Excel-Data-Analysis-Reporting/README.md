## Key Insights

1. **Sales are highly concentrated in a few sub-categories.**
   Phones, Chairs, and Binders together generate the largest share of total
   revenue. Copiers, despite having fewer orders, contribute very high
   profit per order.

2. **Not all high-selling products are profitable.**
   Tables and Bookcases sell well but show consistent negative profit,
   indicating heavy discounting. Discounts above 30% correlate strongly
   with losses across all categories.

3. **Regional performance is uneven.**
   The West region generates the highest total sales and profit, while
   the Central region shows the lowest profit margin. The East region's
   Furniture category is running at a loss.

4. **Consumer segment drives the majority of revenue.**
   Consumer orders account for roughly half of total sales, followed by
   Corporate and Home Office. Home Office customers buy fewer units but
   with higher average order value.

5. **Discount strategy needs review.**
   Discounts above 40% almost always result in a net loss. High-discount
   orders are concentrated in the Furniture category, explaining its
   negative overall profit.
